QC CHECKS (what to look for):
------------------------------------------------------------
1. FIXEL COUNT: WM voxels should have 1-3 fixels (mean ~1.2-2.0)
   - If max > 5: possible noisy FODs or bad masking
   - If mean < 1.0: poor FOD estimation
2. AFD (Apparent Fiber Density): should be positive in WM
   - Outlier subjects (>2 SD from group mean) suggest issues
   - Very low AFD: signal dropout or bad normalization
3. PEAK AMPLITUDE: FOD peak values at fixel directions
   - Should correlate with AFD
4. COMPLEXITY: fraction of voxels with crossing fibers
   - Expected range 0.1-0.4 in mouse brain
   - Very high (>0.5): noisy FODs
5. MASK COVERAGE: fixels should fill the brain mask
   - Voxels with fixels / mask voxels should be >80%
6. VISUAL CHECK in mrview:
   - fixel plot: sticks should align with known WM tracts
   - No fixels in CSF/outside brain
   - Crossing fiber regions should show 2-3 fixels
